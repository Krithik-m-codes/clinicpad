# ClinicPad — Market Research

**Date:** 2026-11-06
**Method:** public pricing pages, vendor comparisons, and category reviews. Prices change frequently; verify before using in sales material.

---

## 1. Executive summary

The Indian clinic-software market is crowded but **not commoditised at the top or the bottom**:

- **Enterprise/hospital** systems are expensive and overbuilt for a 1–5 doctor clinic.
- **EMR-first** products (HealthPlix, Eka, Practo Ray) are strong on clinical records and ABDM, but still force a workflow and a layout.
- **Cheap clinic suites** (MedPrax, Medplix, MyClinicDesk, OPD Suite) compete on price (₹0–₹2,500/month) and breadth, but differentiate weakly on the doctor's actual prescription artifact.
- **International PMSs** (Cliniko, SimplePractice, DrChrono) are 10–30× more expensive and not built for Indian workflows (WhatsApp, ABDM, GST, regional languages, offline).

**The gap:** nobody is selling *"keep your own prescription pad, digitised in 10 minutes, and run the clinic around it — offline."* That is ClinicPad's wedge.

**Pricing reality:** the volume market is ₹500–₹1,500/month per clinic. Multi-doctor clinics pay ₹2,500–₹5,000/month. Annual prepay is the norm. A premium solo product at ₹799–₹999/month is viable if it demonstrably saves time and looks better; a ₹499 anchor is safer for acquisition.

## 2. Competitive landscape — India

### 2.1 Practo Ray

- **What it is:** clinic management inside Practo's ecosystem; strong patient discovery marketplace.
- **Pricing:** reported ~₹5,000–₹15,000/month depending on plan/negotiation; enterprise "Insta" is custom.
- **Strengths:** brand trust, patient acquisition, 50,000+ doctors, ABDM compliant, appointments, records, billing.
- **Weaknesses:** legacy UX, heavy click paths, marketplace-first incentives, less depth in day-to-day clinic operations, limited template flexibility.
- **Lesson for ClinicPad:** discovery is not our game. Partner or stay independent; win on speed and the pad.

### 2.2 HealthPlix

- **What it is:** EMR-first prescription platform for doctors.
- **Pricing:** Pro ~₹12,000/year, Elite ~₹18,000/year (excl. 18% GST); ~₹1,000–₹1,500/month effective.
- **Strengths:** fast prescription workflow, large doctor base, AI scribe (HALO), Plix AI, 14 languages, Smart Scan for lab reports, NABH.
- **Weaknesses:** less owner-grade business tooling; prescription layout is product-defined; pricing has crept up.
- **Lesson:** prescription speed is table stakes. We must match it and win on template ownership + offline + owner tools.

### 2.3 Eka Care

- **What it is:** ABDM-first clinic + patient platform.
- **Pricing:** Eka Doc Plus ₹16,999/year; Eka Doc Pro ₹18,749–₹24,999/year; Clinic Pro ~₹1,00,000/year for 5 doctors + 15 staff; add-on seats ~₹8,000–₹18,749.
- **Strengths:** ABHA/ABDM depth, free tier, e-lab, teleconsultation, patient app, specialty Rx pads.
- **Weaknesses:** operational depth for daily clinic management; storage/teleconsult add-ons; ABDM-centric value may not resonate with a paper-first doctor.
- **Lesson:** ABDM matters for credibility and enterprise, but it is not the wedge for a Tier 2/3 solo doctor. Build it in Phase 5, not Phase 1.

### 2.4 MedPrax OPD

- **What it is:** modern OPD app for Indian clinics.
- **Pricing:** free forever (1 doctor, 150 visits/month); Pro ₹333/month (₹3,999/year); Clinic ₹833/month (₹9,999/year, up to 5 doctors).
- **Strengths:** transparent pricing, offline support, WhatsApp from the clinic's own number with no per-message fee, 14 languages, picture dosage, QR patient cards, no lock-in on downgrade, India (Mumbai) data.
- **Weaknesses:** newer, smaller brand; fewer integrations; prescription design flexibility unclear.
- **Lesson:** this is the closest pricing/positioning competitor. We must be clearly better on template fidelity, owner analytics, documents, and desktop polish. Their "no per-message fee" is a trust signal we should copy.

### 2.5 Medplix

- **What it is:** full clinic suite (appointments, EMR, Rx, billing, lab, pharmacy, HR).
- **Pricing:** ₹499/month + GST, billed annually (~₹5,988/year).
- **Strengths:** very low price, broad module coverage, flat pricing, multiple role logins, owner dashboard.
- **Weaknesses:** breadth may dilute depth; compliance/security maturity unclear; template flexibility unclear.
- **Lesson:** breadth at ₹499 is a real threat. ClinicPad should not try to match every module on day one; win the prescription + owner workflow and add modules sequentially.

### 2.6 OPD Suite

- **Pricing:** Starter ₹999/month (1 doctor, 3 staff, 1 location); Clinic ₹2,499/month (5 doctors, 15 staff, 2 locations); Multi-speciality ₹5,999/month (unlimited doctors, 10 locations). 3 months free; annual = 2 months free.
- **Strengths:** per-clinic pricing, modules, ABDM/ABHA in top tier, dental pack, white-label printing, teleconsult, insurance/TPA, inventory.
- **Weaknesses:** tiered feature gating; higher entry price; generic positioning.
- **Lesson:** per-clinic pricing is attractive to owners. We can use "per clinic, not per doctor" as a differentiator on the Clinic plan.

### 2.7 MedKyo

- **Pricing:** Free; Starter ₹999/month; Professional ₹1,999/month; Enterprise custom.
- **Strengths:** AI prescriptions in ~30 seconds, WhatsApp sharing, GST billing, ABHA, queue, analytics, multi-location, DPDP-compliant claims.
- **Weaknesses:** AI-first positioning may raise trust questions; newer entrant.
- **Lesson:** AI is becoming table stakes. Ship it as an assistant, not the headline.

### 2.8 MyClinicDesk

- **Pricing:** ₹499/month or ₹4,999/year; 14-day free trial; setup service priced separately.
- **Strengths:** simple, India-focused, WhatsApp automation, part payments, self-registration via QR/WhatsApp.
- **Weaknesses:** narrower feature set; less clinical depth.
- **Lesson:** simple onboarding and transparent pricing win. Their setup service validates a paid concierge motion.

### Implications for ClinicPad

- **Free tier is expected.** Use it for acquisition, capped by prescriptions or branding.
- **₹499/month annual** is the psychological anchor. **₹799–₹999/month** is defensible with better templates and owner tools, but requires proof.
- **Per-clinic pricing** is a strong message for multi-doctor clinics. Don't punish them with per-doctor seat math until the Chain tier.
- **Annual prepay** improves cash flow and retention; offer 2 months free.
- **No message markup.** Charge at cost + a small credit pack; publish rates.
- **Setup service** (₹999–₹2,999) is a legitimate revenue line and improves activation.

## 6. What customers actually complain about

Synthesised from reviews, comparison pages, and vendor positioning:

1. **"It doesn't print prescriptions the way I want."** Layout fidelity and letterhead control are decisive.
2. **"Too many clicks."** Prescription speed is the #1 workflow metric.
3. **"It stops working when the internet does."** Offline is a real requirement in Tier 2/3.
4. **"Hidden costs."** WhatsApp credits, storage, per-seat add-ons, renewal hikes.
5. **"Support doesn't understand my clinic."** Onboarding and local-language support matter.
6. **"I can't get my data out."** Lock-in fears block switching.
7. **"The owner can't see the money."** Revenue leakage and outstanding balances are top owner pains.
8. **"ABDM is confusing."** Doctors want compliance without becoming integrators.
9. **"My staff won't learn it."** Reception usability decides adoption.
10. **"AI makes things up."** Trust and clinician review are essential.

## 7. Strategic conclusions

### Where to play

- **Beachhead:** solo/small clinics in Tier 2/3 India, starting with general medicine and paediatrics.
- **Wedge:** BYOP template studio + offline + WhatsApp.
- **Expansion:** appointments, billing, owner analytics, multi-branch.
- **Long-term:** white-label/reseller + AI documentation + ecosystem.

### How to win

1. **Demo in 5 minutes.** Upload a pad → print a real prescription. This is the entire sales pitch.
2. **Be visibly cheaper than EMRs, visibly better than cheap suites.** ₹499–₹999 solo, ₹1,499–₹2,499 clinic.
3. **Own the owner's dashboard.** Revenue, leakage, doctor performance.
4. **Make offline a headline.** "Your clinic doesn't stop when the internet does."
5. **Build a reseller army.** Local IT vendors and medical reps need recurring revenue.
6. **Publish pricing and message rates.** Trust is a differentiator.
7. **Offer done-for-you onboarding.** Charge for it; it funds support and improves retention.

### What not to do

- Don't build a patient marketplace.
- Don't chase hospital IPD/OT.
- Don't claim ABDM/HIPAA compliance before certification.
- Don't compete on "most modules" at ₹499.
- Don't make AI the headline before trust is earned.
- Don't price per doctor for small clinics.
